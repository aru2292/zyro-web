ZYRO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ZYRO ("the Bot") is a Discord application developed by ZYRO DEVELOPMENT. Support: discord.gg/PeA5ReBEY.

1. What the Bot processes

  • Discord identifiers: user, server, channel, role and message IDs needed to run commands and protection features.
  • Settings you configure: prefixes, whitelists, antinuke and automod rules, welcome and goodbye messages, voice-channel settings, role rules, log channels, playlists you save.
  • Voice state: which voice channel a member is in, only to run voice features (temporary channels, voice roles, voice guard).
  • Message content: read only to recognise commands and to run features you enable (for example auto-nickname or auto-responder channels). Messages are not used to train AI models and are not sold.
  • Music requests: the search text or link you send, plus the title, artist, duration and thumbnail of the track. Audio is fetched from YouTube or SoundCloud for playback only.

2. Why we process it

To provide the features you use: moderation, antinuke protection, roles, music, tickets, logs, premium features and support.

3. Storage and retention

  • Data is stored in a database on the Bot's hosting server.
  • Server data is kept while the Bot is in the server. When the Bot is removed, that server's settings are deleted within 30 days.
  • Database backups are made every 12 hours for disaster recovery and are deleted after 30 days.
  • Developer command logs contain command names, user and channel IDs, and command text (up to 160 characters).

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with Discord (the platform), YouTube or SoundCloud (only the query or link needed to play music), and the hosting provider that runs the Bot.

5. Your choices

  • Server owners can remove the Bot, reset its settings, or request deletion through the support server.
  • Users can request deletion of their personal data through the support server.

6. Children

The Bot follows Discord's minimum age (13). We do not knowingly collect data from younger users.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.

8. Contact

Ask in the support server: discord.gg/PeA5ReBEY.
